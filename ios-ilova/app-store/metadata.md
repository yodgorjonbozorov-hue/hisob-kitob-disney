## Description (English)

```
Balansa is a business management app for small and medium businesses in
Uzbekistan. Income and expenses, cash registers, inventory, sales, customer
debts, payroll and reports live in one place instead of scattered notebooks
and spreadsheets.

NOTE: the app interface is in Uzbek (Latin script).

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Income and expenses — record every transaction with category, payment
  method (cash, card, debt) and the employee who entered it.
• Cash registers — keep separate balances per register, transfer between
  them, and close the shift by counting cash. Differences are visible
  immediately, not at the end of the month.
• Inventory — products, purchase prices, stock levels, stock-taking and
  write-offs. Sales reduce stock automatically.
• Debts — customer debts are tracked as obligations, not as income, so your
  profit figure stays honest. Partial payments are supported.
• Daily report — cashiers submit the day's takings; the director confirms.
• Reports — monthly summary, category breakdown, trends, export to Excel
  and PDF.
• Roles — owner, administrator, cashier and seller each see only what they
  need. Custom roles with granular permissions are available on the PRO plan.
• Multiple businesses — run several companies from one account and switch
  between them.

BUILT FOR IPHONE

• Face ID lock — an extra lock on top of your password, so financial data
  stays private even if the phone is unlocked.
• Reports are saved and shared through the native iOS share sheet.
• Works offline-tolerant: a clear screen instead of a blank page when the
  connection drops.

ACCOUNT AND DATA

Each company's data is strictly isolated — one company can never see
another's records. Backups run daily. You can delete your account and all
of its data from inside the app: Settings → Delete account.

An account is required. Subscription and billing are managed outside the
app; the app itself does not sell anything.
```

### Notes (tekshiruvchiga izoh) — SHU MATNNI QO'YING

```
LANGUAGE
The app interface is in Uzbek (Latin script), the language of our users in
Uzbekistan. The App Store does not offer Uzbek as a localization option, so
this listing is in English. A screen-by-screen English walkthrough is below.

DEMO ACCOUNT
The demo account is pre-filled with sample data (transactions, products,
debts, reports) so every screen can be reviewed without entering data.

SCREEN GUIDE (Uzbek -> English)
- "Asosiy"          = Dashboard
- "Yozuvlar"        = Transactions (income / expense list)
- "Kassalar"        = Cash registers
- "Ombor"           = Inventory
- "Sotuv"           = Sales
- "Qarzlar"         = Customer debts
- "Kunlik hisobot"  = Daily report
- "Hisobot"         = Monthly report
- "Sozlamalar"      = Settings

ACCOUNT DELETION (Guideline 5.1.1(v))
Account deletion is available inside the app:
  Sozlamalar (Settings) -> "Hisobni o'chirish" (Delete account)
The owner types the company name to confirm. Access is closed immediately;
all company data is permanently erased after a 30-day cancellation window.
The flow requires no email, phone call or website visit.

PAYMENTS (Guideline 3.1.1)
The app contains NO purchase flow and NO link to any external purchase.
Balansa is a B2B service sold to companies outside the app; inside the iOS
app only the current subscription status is displayed. The demo account has
an active subscription, so no billing screen appears.

NATIVE FUNCTIONALITY
- Face ID / Touch ID app lock (Settings -> "Ilova qulfi")
- Native share sheet for exported PDF/Excel reports
- Haptic feedback on financial confirmations
- Offline screen when the connection drops

DATA ISOLATION
Each company's data is isolated at the database layer; the demo account
cannot see any other customer's records.
```
